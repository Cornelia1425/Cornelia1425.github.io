03 — THE THREAD BETWEEN THINGS

I think with
my whole body.

↳

I’m an artist, filmmaker, dancer, violinist, and builder of things. Architecture taught me to think in space. Dance taught me to listen.

Originally from China and based in New York, I work between live performance, film, and creative technology. I’m interested in how a body holds memory, how a place changes a story, and how making things together brings us closer.

Through Summer Breeze, I create space for dancers and musicians to share what they make—bringing the energy of sessions and clubs onto the stage.
